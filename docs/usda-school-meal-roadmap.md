# School meal planning: USDA review goal

DietLedger's goal is to help schools plan nutritious, satisfying meals for children. Following beta, we intend to investigate and pursue USDA evaluation and approval as **National School Lunch Program (NSLP) meal pattern assessment software** for certification of compliance, subject to USDA's eligibility and review requirements.

**This is a future goal, not an existing approval.** DietLedger is beta software and is not currently USDA-approved meal pattern assessment software. Schools must independently verify meal patterns, nutrition standards, allergies, food safety, quantities, and operational suitability.

## What we plan to do

1. Confirm the current USDA application, evaluation, eligibility, and data requirements directly with USDA.
2. Identify and implement any missing meal-pattern assessment functionality, then validate it against authoritative requirements and school workflows.
3. After beta, submit DietLedger for USDA review when it is ready and eligible. Approval is not guaranteed.
4. If approval is obtained, plan for annual submissions and review to maintain it, while confirming the actual ongoing requirements and schedule with USDA. Do not assume that annual review is the same as an automatically renewable annual certificate.

USDA states that companies using its Child Nutrition Database for approved nutrient-analysis software must continue to submit their software annually for review; this does not by itself establish the renewal terms for meal-pattern approval. USDA also distinguishes software approval from a state agency's certification of a school food authority. USDA says its meal-pattern assessment software evaluation does not cover the breakfast meal pattern; states may authorize breakfast use.
