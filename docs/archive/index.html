One connected food workflow

Less bouncing between tools.
More seeing the whole picture.

DietLedger is built around how food decisions actually connect. A recipe can become a planned meal, a grocery list, and a food log—not a dead end in another app.

01▤

Recipes with nutrition attached

Build and organize recipes, inspect nutrition, and preserve published cookbook nutrition with its source and missing-data context. Keep the recipe meaningful even when ingredient details come from a cookbook.

Build your personal recipe library
02◉

Filters with context—not guesswork

Explore recipes against editable nutrition targets. The optional Diabetes-aware starter profile can help narrow discovery; it is a screening aid, not a safety certification or treatment plan.

Unknown nutrition is not a match
03▦

A plan that becomes a list

Plan recipes across days, then turn planned servings into a grocery list that scales and combines ingredients. Start shopping with a list shaped by the meals you actually intend to make.

Plan → shop → cook
04◷

Track the pattern over time

Log food and follow nutrition and health metrics alongside your planning workflow. Keep the information that supports your own decisions in one self-operated workspace.

Your server. Your stewardship.

Self-hosted by design.
Not a rented account.

DietLedger is software you deploy and maintain—not a hosted subscription. Run it with Docker Compose and PostgreSQL, manage your own updates and backups, and decide who can reach your instance.

  • Build and run the app with Docker Compose
  • Keep the database on infrastructure you administer
  • Export recipes and books; plan separate full-database backups
See setup steps and requirements →

Important: Self-hosting means you are responsible for server security, access controls, HTTPS, updates, and tested data backups. It is not a managed service.
