The Completed Recipe Collection

A practical, welcoming cookbook arranged to move from breakfast and baking through soups, mains, useful staples, and sweets. The chapter-and-section plan below places every recipe found in seed-recipes/complete/ exactly once. Recipe names and the short notes/timing details are drawn from those files; this is an organizational proposal, not a replacement recipe source.

Contents: 303 completed seed recipes. Incomplete seeds and generated bundles are excluded.

Erwtensoep (Dutch pea soup)

Erwtensoep, also called snert, is the Dutch version of pea soup. Slices of rookworst (smoked sausage) are added before serving. The soup, which is traditionally eaten during the winter, is emblematic of Dutch cuisine. It is customarily served with Frisian rye bread (roggebrood) and bacon, cheese or butter. Pancakes are sometimes served with pea soup; this dish is called snert met struif, struif referring to the pancakes.
